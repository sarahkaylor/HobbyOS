## Anomaly, stated rather than smoothed

The log is not one monotonic `[XSW]` sequence: alongside the run-long stream
there are rows with their own clock (`t` 0..25 269 ms) and counter (n 1..80)
carrying the loader-art CRC, starting after the modal appeared. One
`rwlaunch: exec` and one `Initialize engine version` are in the log, so it is
one game process with two present streams, not a second launch. Every window
above is restricted to the run-long stream (`t > 600 000`), and the frame CRCs
quoted are from it; the menu frame itself is the byte-identical pair above.
